The blockout comes first and is built in measured passes: landmarks or a proportion skeleton with real dimensions and a 1.8 m scale reference, then primary masses with gesture and taper (extruded profiles or graded skin volumes, not stacked default primitives), then secondary forms and deliberate negative space, then the part and pivot hierarchy with every moving part range-tested; each pass prints or states its measured numbers
